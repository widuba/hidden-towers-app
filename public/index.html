How to play

Four steps to victory

Move one tile at a time. No diagonals, no clutter — just clean, tense, turn-by-turn strategy.

1

Explore

Move up, down, left or right to reveal the hidden grid. Each new tile is +10 points.

2

Claim towers

Step onto a hidden tower to claim it — it turns your color and banks +50 points.

3

Upgrade & defend

Stay on your tower to level it up — all the way to Level 8 and +1,342 points. Guard it well.

4

Win

Hit the point goal or control enough towers before your rivals do. Or invade theirs in 5 turns.
