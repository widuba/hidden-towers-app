How to Play
🏰 Hidden Towers

A fog-of-war strategy board game. Explore a hidden grid, capture towers, upgrade them, outmaneuver opponents, and race to victory before the board turns against you.

▶ How a Turn Works

On your turn, move one tile in any direction — or stay where you are. Players take turns one at a time.

↑ Up ↓ Down ← Left → Right S — Stay
No diagonal movement. You can only move to directly adjacent tiles.

What happens when you step on a tile

  • Hidden tile — it reveals and you earn +10 points.
  • Empty tile — nothing happens; you just move there.
  • Tower — triggers the tower interaction.
  • Infected tile — you become infected. Your next turn you are teleported to a random tile instead of choosing your move.
  • Safe infected tile — safe for you if you own the protecting tower; otherwise still dangerous.

🏛 Towers

Towers are the heart of the game. Claiming, upgrading, and defending towers is how you score the most points and win.

Claiming a Tower

Towers start hidden. Walk onto an undiscovered tower to claim it — it turns your color and you earn points for a Level 1 tower (+50 pts).

Upgrading Your Tower

Stay on your own tower turn after turn to upgrade it. It takes tower level stay-turns to reach the next level. Each upgrade earns you points.

Example: a Level 3 tower needs 3 stay-turns to reach Level 4.

Capturing an Enemy Tower

Walk onto an enemy tower to start invading it. Stay on it for 5 consecutive turns to capture it. You earn points for the tower at its current level.

The invasion bar fills turn by turn:

3 of 5 turns done — 2 more to capture.

If the owner steps back on their tower while you are invading, the invasion resets to zero.

🌙 Blackout & Eclipse

Blackout

In Blackout, revealed tiles do not stay revealed forever. After a revealed tile goes 3 turns without any player stepping on it, it becomes hidden again.

◼ Tiles Vanish

Any revealed tile can become hidden again after being ignored for 3 turns.

🏛 Towers Can Hide Too

A tower can disappear back into the fog, but it keeps its owner and level.

↻ Progress Resets

If a tower hides again, upgrade progress and takeover progress reset to zero.

🧠 Memory Matters

Remember where important towers were, because the board can go dark again.

Eclipse

Eclipse includes all Blackout rules, but adds a bigger twist: every 10 moves, the hidden part of the board shifts.

Only hidden tiles shift. Revealed tiles stay where they are. Hidden towers can move, but their owner and level stay the same.
☣ Poison Gas

In Poison Gas mode, a hidden gas tile exists somewhere on the board. Once any player walks onto it, gas begins spreading at the end of each full round.

Infected tiles are permanent. If you step on one, your next turn you are teleported to a random tile instead of moving normally.

Safe Lanes

After gas finishes spreading, each owned tower carves a safe lane through infected tiles. The lane runs in all four cardinal directions from the tower to the edge of the board.

Safe lanes are shown on the board with the owner's color and letter. Other players still get infected walking through them.

🧠 1 Player vs AI

Select 1 Player when creating a room to play solo against a CPU opponent. The AI is available for all 2-player game modes.

Easy
Makes random moves 40% of the time. Looks only 2 moves ahead. Good for learning the game.
Medium
Looks up to 4 moves ahead with a 250 ms time limit. Plays well but makes mistakes.
Hard
Uses deeper search with alpha-beta pruning and iterative deepening. Very difficult to beat.
The AI always plays as Player 2. The host device runs the AI calculations — it plays automatically after a short delay.

💡 Strategy Tips
🔍 Reveal early

Uncover tiles fast at the start to find towers before your opponents and bank easy points.

🔴 Defend high-level towers

A Level 7 or 8 tower is worth a lot of points. Do not leave them unguarded.

☣ Plan around gas

In Poison Gas, tower placement determines your safe lanes after gas finalizes.

🔥 Upgrade carefully

A higher-level tower is powerful, but it also becomes a bigger target.

🕐 Timing invasions

Capture attempts take 5 turns. Start invading only when you can commit.

🎯 Tower vs points race

In 3–4 player modes, owning 4 towers can win outright.

🌙 Remember the map

In Blackout and Eclipse, important tiles can vanish again. Try to remember where towers were.

🌑 Watch the Eclipse timer

In Eclipse, hidden tiles shift every 10 moves. Revealed information is safe, but hidden towers may move.
